# Getting started with ParcelPilot

ParcelPilot is a parcel-tracking service for small online stores. This guide covers
account setup, tracking a parcel, and delivery notifications.

## Creating your account

1. Sign up at parcelpilot.example.com with your work email.
2. Verify your email within 24 hours — unverified accounts cannot create shipping labels.
3. Add your warehouse pickup address under Settings → Addresses.

## Tracking a parcel

Every ParcelPilot shipment gets a tracking ID in the format `PP-XXXXXX` (for example,
`PP-482913`). You can track a parcel three ways:

- Paste the tracking ID at parcelpilot.example.com/track.
- Use the tracking link in your shipping confirmation email.
- Ask the support chatbot on this page — it can look up tracking steps from this guide.

Tracking updates appear in near real time: label created → picked up → in transit →
out for delivery → delivered.

## Delivery notifications

Email and SMS notifications are free on all plans. Enable them under
Settings → Notifications. SMS alerts are sent at pickup, out-for-delivery, and
delivery events only — we never send marketing texts.

## What if my tracking hasn't updated in 48 hours?

First, check that the tracking ID is correct — a single wrong digit is the most
common cause. If the ID is correct and there is no update after 48 hours, contact
support; the parcel may be delayed at a sorting hub.
