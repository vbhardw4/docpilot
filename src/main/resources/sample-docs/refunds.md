# Refunds and claims for lost or damaged parcels

## Filing a claim

If your parcel is lost or arrives damaged, file a claim within **30 days of the
ship date**. Claims filed after 30 days are automatically denied — no exceptions.

To file a claim:

1. Go to parcelpilot.example.com/claims and enter the tracking ID.
2. Describe the damage or loss and attach photos (for damage claims).
3. Submit. You will receive a claim reference number by email within 1 business day.

## What is covered

- **Lost parcels:** full refund of the shipping cost plus the declared parcel value,
  up to a maximum of $500 per parcel.
- **Damaged parcels:** refund of the shipping cost plus repair or replacement value,
  up to a maximum of $500 per parcel, when photos show the damage clearly.
- **Late delivery:** if a Priority overnight parcel arrives late, the shipping cost
  is refunded automatically — no claim needed.

## Refund timeline

Approved refunds are issued to the **original payment method** within **5–10
business days** of claim approval. You will receive an email when the refund is
processed.

## What is NOT covered

- Parcels with no tracking ID (for example, labels bought outside ParcelPilot).
- Damage to items packed without adequate padding (less than 5 cm of cushioning).
- Delays caused by customs inspections or extreme weather.
- Claims filed more than 30 days after the ship date.
