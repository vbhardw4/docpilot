# Shipping rates and delivery windows

All prices are in Canadian dollars (CAD) and include fuel surcharges. Rates apply to
parcels up to 5 kg; heavier parcels are quoted at label-creation time.

## Service levels

| Service             | Price  | Delivery window                    |
|---------------------|--------|------------------------------------|
| Standard            | $8.99  | 3–5 business days                  |
| Express             | $16.99 | 1–2 business days                  |
| Priority overnight  | $29.99 | Next business day (order by 3pm ET)|

## Coverage

- **Standard** covers all Canadian provinces and territories.
- **Express** covers Ontario, Quebec, British Columbia, and Alberta only.
- **Priority overnight** covers the Greater Toronto Area and Montreal metro only.

Remote and northern postal codes may add 1–2 business days to any service level;
the exact estimate is shown before you buy the label.

## Cutoff times

Order by **3:00 PM Eastern Time** for same-day pickup on business days. Orders placed
after the cutoff, or on weekends and public holidays, are picked up the next
business day.

## Can I change the delivery address after shipping?

Yes, but only before the parcel is marked "out for delivery". Address changes cost
$4.99 and are requested from the tracking page. Once a parcel is out for delivery,
the address cannot be changed.
